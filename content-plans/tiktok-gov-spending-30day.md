# TikTok 30-Day Content Plan: Government Spending Facts

**Goal:** authority + community (trust, saves, comments, follows from people who want the receipts)
**Window:** Mon 2026-11-02 to Sun 2026-11-29 (4 weeks)
**Cadence:** 4 posts/week (Mon, Tue, Thu, Sat), 16 posts total
**Format:** faceless: voiceover + screen recordings of primary documents, on-screen numbers, captions burned in

## Positioning

"Every number has a source on screen." The account's edge is verifiability, not outrage. Viewers should be able to pause on any frame and find the document.

## Sourcing rules (non-negotiable for this niche)

1. Every figure comes from a primary source shown on screen: USAspending.gov, Treasury Fiscal Data, CBO, GAO, agency Inspector General reports, Congress.gov, SAM.gov/FPDS contract records.
2. State the fiscal year, whether the number is obligations, outlays, or appropriations, and whether it is nominal or inflation-adjusted.
3. Separate fact from opinion in the script. Facts get a source card; opinion is labeled "my take" or left out.
4. Never imply a program is fraud or waste unless an audit, IG, or court finding says so, and then name that finding and its date.
5. Put the full source list in the caption or pinned comment. Keep a `sources` row per video in the tracker below.
6. Correct errors publicly with a pinned comment and an edit or re-post. Say so in the next video.

## Content pillars

| Pillar | Share | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| A. "Where the money actually goes" | 35% | Simple breakdowns, scale comparisons |
| B. "Receipts": one contract/grant, traced | 30% | Shows method, builds authority |
| C. "Audit says" (GAO/IG findings) | 20% | Documented problems, with the finding quoted |
| D. "Myth vs. number" | 15% | Community-driven, answers comment questions |

## Recurring series (so viewers know what to expect)

- **"Follow the Dollar"**: one award traced on USAspending, start to finish (Pillar B)
- **"Audit Says"**: 45s on one GAO/IG finding, quote on screen (Pillar C)
- **"Scale Check"**: turns a huge number into per-person or per-day terms (Pillar A)
- **"You Asked"**: answers a top comment with a sourced number (Pillar D)

## Video format template (35-60s)

- 0-2s: hook as on-screen text + first VO line (a specific number or a question)
- 2-10s: context: what the number is and what it is not
- 10-40s: 2-3 beats, each with a source card on screen
- 40-55s: takeaway + "Source is pinned. What should I trace next?"
- Caption: 1 sentence + sources + 3-5 hashtags. Pin a comment with links.

## Production workflow (batching)

- **Sunday:** pick the week's 4 topics, pull sources, fill the tracker below
- **Monday:** write all 4 scripts (about 110-150 words each), record VO in one session
- **Tuesday:** screen-record source pages, assemble edits, burn captions
- **Daily 15 min:** reply to comments within the first hour of posting; note questions for "You Asked"

## Engagement tactics

- End every video with one specific question tied to the topic (not "thoughts?")
- Reply to top comments with video replies (counts toward "You Asked")
- Pin the sources comment; reply to "source?" with the link plus the page/table
- Keep a visible, consistent look: same font, same source-card design, same color for "fact" vs. "my take"

## Risks and guardrails

- **Misleading framing:** big numbers without a denominator mislead. Always give scale (per person, % of total).
- **Stale data:** USAspending and agency data are revised. Re-check the figure the day you publish.
- **Partisan drift:** keep the focus on documents and findings, so authority doesn't depend on the audience's politics.
- **Defamation:** don't name individuals or companies in connection with wrongdoing unless a public finding does, and cite it.
- **Platform rules:** TikTok's election and civic-integrity policies apply to government-related content around political events; avoid unverified claims about elections or officials.

## Open decisions

2. VO: your own voice or an AI voice (TikTok requires labeling of AI-generated content)
