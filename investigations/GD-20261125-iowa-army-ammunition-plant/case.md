## Neutral brief
The document is EPA Region 7's final RCRA hazardous waste permit, issued in September 2018 to the U.S. Army (owner) and American Ordnance LLC (operator) for the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant. The plant loads, assembles and packs military munitions. The permit:
- authorizes hazardous waste container storage;
- authorizes open burning (OB) and open detonation (OD) of reactive and explosive wastes, including a 40mm grenade OD unit;
- covers post-closure care of the Trench 5 Ash Disposal Cell;
- covers corrective action at solid waste management units.

The packet opens with EPA's Response to Comments: seven comments, from a public commenter, Iowa DNR's Air Quality Bureau and the permittees. Past releases at IAAP are stated to be handled under EPA's Superfund program. The permit runs for up to 10 years from issuance (about September 2028).

This case reviews the document for red flags and inconsistencies, places it in the context of the site's contamination and regulatory history, and profiles American Ordnance LLC and its federal funding.

## Second document and user clarifications (added 2026-11-25)
The user supplied the EPA Region 7 **Emergency Hazardous Waste Management Facility Permit for Storage and Treatment of Lead Azide** (S2), effective 13 Nov 2026 to 11 Feb 2027. It is redacted. It authorizes the Army to detonate 68 drums of recrystallized lead azide in place inside Earth Covered Magazines (ECMs), because the material's sensitivity to friction and shock now makes it "highly unstable and dangerous". The user asked us to clarify two points:
- whether the grenade unit is **40mm or 44mm** ("updated permit with mixed details");
