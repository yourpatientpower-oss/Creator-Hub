# Claim Auditor — GD-20261120-deutsche-bank-italy

Module owner: claim-auditor. Written 2026-11-20. Sources are cited by ledger ID (`sources.md`). Claim IDs C1-C18 come from `case.md` (C17-C18 are a user lead added mid-audit; see section 2c). C19-C23 are **additions** made by this module and are flagged as such.

**How the research was done (read this first).** The Nimble Web Search Agent could not be used (billing error, `trialNoQuota`). Research used about 17 Nimble Search queries in English, Italian and German, plus one Nimble Extract of a primary PDF (S17). Except for S17, nearly every news source was read **through search-result text only**, not a full page fetch. The FT originals (S8, and the FT Epstein and Trump pieces) are paywalled. ## 1. Question investigated

What material claims are made about (a) hidden losses at Deutsche Bank S.p.A. (C1-C7), (b) the Epstein and Trump strands of the "Inside Deutsche Bank" leak (C8-C11, C16), and (c) Paolo Zampolli (C12-C14)? Does the evidence support each one? Priority went to C1-C7 and to the user's two implied premises: C12 (Zampolli is connected to the leak or the Italian unit) and C15 (the Italian-unit losses are connected to Epstein and/or Trump).

### 2b. Key findings (narrative)

1. **Italian-unit matter (C1-C7).** Every substantive detail traces to one origin: the FT's 19 Nov 2026 reading of leaked internal documents (S8). Italian and German outlets relay that reporting and add no independent sourcing (S9-S16). DB's on-record response (S3, S16, S9) does not dispute the facts. It reframes them as a reassessment of funding assumptions after Italian rule changes, reported to supervisors, needing no restatement and immaterial at group level. Two points cut against the strongest framing of C1. The crystallised loss was €522m, with €779m as a potential total (S12). The internal probe reportedly found negligence or incompetence, not intentional fraud (C19). This is a reported allegation and not a regulatory finding. No supervisor has made a public finding (C21).
2. **Primary-source confirmation (C5/C22).** The FT's "~€1bn capital injection" is borne out by DB S.p.A.'s own 2022 financial statements: €1.0bn across 2018-2021 (S17). The filing gives other stated purposes (IFRS 9 transition; a €168.2m loss on early termination of intra-group deposits). So the amount is confirmed, but the filing does not show that the injections covered the Aetos losses. The intra-group deposit-termination loss *may* relate to the "funding assumptions" the bank mentions. That is my **hypothesis only**, not a finding.
3. **Zampolli (C12).** No source found, in English, Italian or German, places Zampolli in the Inside Deutsche Bank leak or the Italian-unit matter. His documented exposure is in the **DOJ Epstein files** and the **26 Oct 2026 House Judiciary Democrats' staff report** (S24, S27). Both are separate from the leak. That report is a partisan allegation, Zampolli denies any relationship with Epstein (S25), and the White House rejects the report's claims about Trump (S25). Absence of evidence is not disproof. The leak is not public.
4. **Italian unit and Epstein/Trump (C15).** No source found links them. They are separate chapters of the same leak, with unrelated mechanisms and jurisdictions as reported. One plausible origin of the user's impression is news pages and commentary that bundle these stories together (S14, S29, S30). That is a hypothesis about how the confusion arose, not a finding.
5. **Epstein/Trump items (C8-C11).** These are well corroborated *as reporting* across several consortium partners (FT, Spiegel, Financial Mail and others). The partners share the same leaked documents, so they are not independent of each other on the underlying facts. DB has confirmed the Epstein onboarding error (S19).

## 3. Contrary evidence

- The internal probe reportedly found no intentional fraud or collusion (C19). This contradicts a reading of C1 as deliberate concealment by management. The FT nonetheless reports a hidden reserve used to hit profit targets (C2), so the two characterisations sit in tension. **Unresolved.**
- DB says the matter was reported to supervisors, reviewed with the auditor, and required no restatement or group-level disclosure (C7). If accurate, "hidden" applies to public and investor disclosure, not to supervisors. The FT says the ECB was told in 2020 (C4).
- The capital injections (C5) have stated purposes in the filing that differ from the FT's implied purpose (S17).
- The Trump SAR memo itself reportedly says Trump was not a SAR suspect and the Baku companies were not DB customers (S22).
- Zampolli denies any relationship with Epstein (S25). The HJC report is a minority staff document (S24), and the White House rejects its claims about Trump (S25).
- Figures vary across relays: €779m vs ~€800m vs "850m" (Il Fatto headline, S12); transaction counts in C8; the end year of the Italian conduct (2016 vs 2018).

## 4. Confidence and limitations

- **C17/C18:** I found no reliable source for Milan property or a Milan agency office, and none for any DB S.p.A. relationship. I did not search the registry (paywalled, and private records are out of scope).
- **Overall confidence: medium.** It is high for what DB said (C7, C16), for the existence and denial of the Zampolli allegation (C14), and for the capital-injection amount (C5/C22). It is medium for leak-derived facts. It is medium for the "no source found" conclusions on C12 and C15, which cover public reporting only.
- Only S17 and the Nimble search texts were read. The FT originals are paywalled and were seen only as snippets. Most other pages were not fully fetched. I did not create Wayback copies.
- The Web Search Agent was unavailable, so coverage relied on about 17 targeted searches. Non-indexed or very recent articles may have been missed.
- The leaked documents are not public, so no leak-derived claim can be checked against the underlying documents.
- I did not obtain DB S.p.A. FY2018-2021/2023 statements, the KPMG audit reports, the NYDFS 2020 consent order, the full HJC report PDF, or any ECB, Banca d'Italia or BaFin release on the Italian matter.
- Executives named in the FT reporting are referred to by role here, because the FT reports they were cleared of personal misconduct.
