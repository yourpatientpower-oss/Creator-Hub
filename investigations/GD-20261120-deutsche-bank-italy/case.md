## Neutral brief
On 17 Nov 2026 a media consortium (FT, Der Spiegel, Le Monde, ZDF, NYT and others) began publishing "Inside Deutsche Bank", based on ~15m leaked internal files. Reported items include Epstein-linked transactions flagged as suspicious (~$582m), a 2017 accidental disclosure of Trump tax documents, suspicious-activity reports on Trump-linked accounts, and Russia-related dealings. On 19 Nov 2026 the FT reported that Deutsche Bank's Italian unit allegedly used derivatives and a hidden reserve ("tesoretto") to conceal mortgage-lending losses of up to ~€800m for over a decade until a 2018 whistleblower report. Deutsche Bank says the matters are historical, were reported to supervisors, required no restatement, and were not material at group level. Separately, a 26 Oct 2026 House Judiciary Democrats report made allegations about Paolo Zampolli's role in Epstein's activities, which he rejects. This case tests what is documented in each strand and whether the strands are actually connected.

## Scope
- In scope: Italian-unit loss allegations and bank response; regulatory/auditor handling; Epstein and Trump items in the leak; any documented link between Zampolli and Deutsche Bank / the leak / the Italian unit; regulatory actions and settlements relevant to these strands.
- Out of scope: Russia/oligarch strand except as context; Zampolli's private life and the civil suit beyond one-line context; the identity of the whistleblowers; any non-public data.
