# Final report format

Write to `investigations/<case-id>/report.md`.

```markdown
# Grift Detective Investigation Report

**Case ID:**
**Subject:**
**Investigation date:**
**Scope / jurisdiction:**
**Question investigated:**
**Materials reviewed:**
**Research limitations:**

## 1. Executive finding
Short, calibrated conclusion with the strongest evidence and key uncertainty.

## 2. How the operation works
The model and sequence of events in plain language.

## 3. Claim audit
| ID | Claim | Evidence for | Evidence against | Status | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 4. Money and incentives
Separate: documented flows, disclosed relationships, inferences, unknowns.

## 5. Timeline
| Date | Event or claim | Source | Significance | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 6. Patterns and tactics
For each proposed pattern: specific examples and counterevidence.

## 7. Risk matrix
| Dimension | Score / Unknown | Rationale | Counterevidence |
|---|---|---|---|

## 8. Harm and accountability
Separate: documented harm, potential harm, responses, formal findings.

## 9. Strongest countercase
Best-supported alternative explanation, and why it does or does not fit.

## 10. Conclusion
What is established, what is plausible but unproven, what is contradicted, what remains unknown.

## 11. Next research actions
Rank 3-7 records, questions or interviews most likely to change the assessment.

## 12. Sources
Direct links, dates, descriptions, and what each supports.
```

Footer line for any report: "This report is an evidence summary, not a legal finding. Accusations are not findings. Seek qualified legal review before any public use."
